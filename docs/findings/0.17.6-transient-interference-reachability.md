# 0.17.6 transient interference reachability

**Decision: transient interference is retained and validated.**

The mechanism stays opt-in (`apply_interference=False` by default). Proactive and retroactive penalties are reachable for clearly competing, temporally ordered payments-api deployment memories, and the negative controls stay at zero penalty. No weight or global threshold was changed. Bench `transient_interference_v1` was not used as the acceptance target.

## What was unreachable

On `How is payments-api deployed?` before the correction:

- Episodes carried an active support slot in `SupportProvenance` but the competition profile left `semantic_slot_key` empty, so episode pairs were not indexed.
- Episode cue fit was lexical only (`text_cue_fit` about `0.28`), so even a same-slot relationship fell under `minimum_strength`.
- The semantic Jenkins / GitHub Actions pair was diagnostic and same-slot, with cue fit `0.50`, but `strength = 0.95 × 0.50 = 0.475` failed `minimum_behavioral_strength` (`0.60`). Cue fit had already passed its own `0.40` gate.

## Correction

Evidence plumbing only:

- An episode competition profile copies `semantic_slot_key` when every active support provenance entry shares one slot. Superseded-only support does not grant that slot.
- That episode's competition cue fit may rise to the cue fit of the semantic it supports. Overlap-only pairs do not receive this lift.
- Same-slot and same-subject/same-predicate pairs compare `relationship_strength` with `minimum_behavioral_strength`. Cue fit remains a separate gate. Pair pressure is still `competition strength × competitor accessibility`.

`HIGH_INTERFERENCE` is absent on those controls. It is present on the positive retrieval when the metamemory pressure threshold is `0.01`, below the measured pressure.

## Persistent inhibition

With `payments-api deployment_system` and `apply_to_recall=True`, inhibiting the older Jenkins semantic lowers its transient contribution to the newer GitHub Actions semantic. The query-level state is `combined`. Persistent inhibition still runs before transient interference.

## Preserved

- `apply_interference=False` leaves negative-control activations unchanged.
- Repeated retrieval and reversed ingest order produce the same episode penalties.
- Penalties are negative-only, and post-interference activation does not rise.
- No storage migration. `010_retrieval_inhibition.sql` remains current.
